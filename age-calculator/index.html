Age Calculator

Free age calculator. Find your exact age in years, months and days, plus your next birthday.

Why TaskFlex built this

Gives your exact age in years, months and days.

How to use it

  1. Choose your date of birth.
  2. Leave the as-of date as today, or change it.
  3. Read your age and next birthday.

Questions

What about 29 February?

In non-leap years the birthday is counted as 28 February.

Is my date saved?

No.

Can I find my age on a past date?

Yes. Change the as-of date.

Does it show my next birthday?

Yes, it shows your next birthday.

Is my date of birth stored?

TaskFlex does not send it anywhere. The sum is done in your browser.

Can I check a child's age?

Yes. Enter the child's birth date.

Why do years, months and days matter?

Some forms ask for the exact age on a cut-off date, which you can find by changing the as-of date.

Who it helps

People filling in forms, checking eligibility for exams or jobs, and families who want to know an exact age in days.

Examples

Enter a birth date to see the age in years, months and days. Change the as-of date to find an age on a past or future date, such as the last date of a form.

Tips

Check that the as-of date is the one the form asks for before you copy the result.

How it works

The tool compares your birth date with the as-of date. It counts the full years first, then the remaining full months, then the remaining days. By default the as-of date is today.

Mistakes to avoid

Entering the date in the wrong order is the usual error. Pick the date from the calendar control and read it back, for example 5 March against 3 May, before you use the answer in a form.
