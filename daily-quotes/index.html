Daily Quotes

A new short quote every day, the same all day. Free daily motivation quotes you can copy and share.

Why TaskFlex built this

One short thought a day, the same all day, new at local midnight.

How to use it

  1. Read today's quote.
  2. Tap Another quote to browse.
  3. Tap Copy to share it.

Questions

When does it change?

At midnight on your device clock.

Are quotes copied from books?

No. They are short original lines written for TaskFlex.

Does the quote change every day?

Yes. A new one appears at your local midnight.

Can I read more than one a day?

Yes. Tap Another quote to browse more.

Can I share the quotes?

They are short lines for personal use. Credit the author when you publish one.

Can I add my own quotes?

Not at the moment. Use the Smart Notepad to keep lines you like.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded it can be saved for offline use.

Who it helps

Anyone who likes a short thought to start the day, and creators who need a line for a caption or a message to a friend.

Examples

Read the quote with your morning tea. Copy it into a status or a note. Tap Another quote when you want a different line.

Tips

The quote of the day stays the same until your local midnight, so you can come back later and find it again. Copy the text instead of taking a screenshot.

How it works

The quote of the day is chosen from the date on your device, so it stays the same until your local midnight. Another quote shows a different line without changing the quote of the day.

Mistakes to avoid

Quotes work best as a short prompt, not as a rule for life. Use them as a caption or a conversation starter, and check the author before you present a line as a famous person's words.
