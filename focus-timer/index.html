Focus Timer

Free Pomodoro timer and countdown with focus and break modes. Accurate even when the tab sleeps.

Why TaskFlex built this

A timer for focused work and breaks, accurate even if the tab sleeps.

How to use it

  1. Pick Focus, Short break, Long break or your own minutes.
  2. Tap Start.
  3. A sound plays when time is up.

Questions

What is Pomodoro?

Work for 25 minutes, rest for 5, and rest longer after four rounds.

Does it keep time in a background tab?

Yes. It uses the clock, not a counter.

Does it keep time if my screen locks?

It is built to stay accurate even if the tab sleeps, but keep the page open so you hear the sound.

Can I change the length?

Yes. Choose your own minutes.

How loud is the sound?

It uses your device volume, so set it before you start.

What is a good study routine?

Many people use 25 minutes of focus and 5 minutes of rest, then a longer break after four rounds. Adjust it to what works for you.

Can I use it for cooking or exercise?

Yes. Set your own minutes for any activity.

Who it helps

Students preparing for exams, people working from home and anyone who finds it hard to stay on one task.

Examples

Run 25 minutes of Focus, then a 5 minute Short break, and repeat. Use a custom time of 50 minutes for a long study block.

Tips

Decide the task before you press Start. Keep the page open and the volume up so you hear the sound when time is up.

How it works

Focus, Short break and Long break are ready-made lengths, and you can type your own minutes. The timer is built to stay accurate even when your phone puts the tab to sleep, and it plays a sound when the time is up.

Mistakes to avoid

Skipping breaks is the usual mistake. A short rest helps you keep attention for the next round, so stand up, drink water and look away from the screen before you start again.
