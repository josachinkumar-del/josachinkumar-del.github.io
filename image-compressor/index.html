Image Compressor

Compress JPG, PNG and WebP photos in your browser. Free, private, nothing is uploaded.

Why TaskFlex built this

Make photos smaller for forms, email and websites. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Choose up to 20 photos.
  2. Pick format, quality and maximum size.
  3. Download each result.

Questions

Is location data removed?

Yes. The new file is redrawn, so EXIF data is not copied.

Why is a file sometimes not smaller?

If the result is bigger, we keep your original.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Compression happens on your device.

Will quality drop?

Some detail is lost when a photo gets smaller, so check the result before you use it.

How many photos can I add?

Up to 20 at a time.

Which format is smallest?

WebP and JPEG are usually smaller than PNG for photos.

Can I compress a screenshot?

Yes, but text in a screenshot can look softer when the quality is low.

Who it helps

Anyone who must upload a photo to a form, job portal or website with a file size limit, and bloggers who want faster pages.

Examples

Reduce a large phone photo to a size a form accepts. Compress up to 20 photos in one go before sending them by email.

Tips

Lower the quality in small steps and check the result before you download. Choose a smaller maximum size if the photo is still too large.

How it works

Your browser redraws each photo at the size and quality you choose and saves a smaller file. This uses features built into the browser, so no photo leaves your device.

Mistakes to avoid

Compressing a photo again and again makes it worse each time. Keep your original and compress it once from there, and do not shrink a photo more than the form needs.
