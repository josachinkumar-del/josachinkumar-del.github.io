TaskFlex

Video Player

Play video links in a clean player with speed, screen ratio and picture controls. Supports video files, YouTube and Vimeo. Remembers where you stopped.

Why TaskFlex built this

Paste a link to a video file, or a YouTube or Vimeo link, and watch it in a clean player with the controls you choose. It remembers where you stopped.

How to use it

  1. Paste the video link and tap Play.
  2. Use speed, screen ratio, fit and picture controls below the video.
  3. Come back later: saved videos appear as cards you can play again or delete.

Questions

Which links work?

Direct links to video files (.mp4, .webm), YouTube and Vimeo. Pages such as Instagram or Facebook do not give direct links, so they cannot be played here.

Can it raise the real quality?

No player can add detail that is not in the video. The picture controls adjust brightness, contrast and colour only. For YouTube, quality is chosen inside its own player.

Is anything saved?

Only the links and your stopping point, on your device. You can delete them any time.

Does it save my place?

Yes. It remembers where you stopped on this device.

Why will my link not play?

The link may not point to a video file, or the website may block playing it elsewhere.

Is my video copied to your servers?

No. TaskFlex does not copy the video anywhere.

Can I change the speed?

Yes. Use the speed control.

Does it work offline?

Only the page itself does. Videos from links need internet.

Who it helps

People who want a clean player for a link to a video file, or for a YouTube or Vimeo link, with controls for speed and screen fit.

Examples

Paste a lecture link and slow it down to follow the steps. Come back tomorrow and carry on from where you stopped.

Tips

Use a direct link to a video file, or a YouTube or Vimeo link. YouTube and Vimeo videos load in their own players, which follow their own privacy rules.

How it works

A direct link plays in the browser's own player, while YouTube and Vimeo links load inside their embedded players. Your saved videos and where you stopped are stored in this browser only.

Mistakes to avoid

Pasting a web page address instead of the video address is the commonest problem. A page address that is not from YouTube or Vimeo may not play. Respect the owner's rights when you watch or share a video.
