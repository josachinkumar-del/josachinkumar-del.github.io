Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for TaskFlex, the free private tools website.

1. Who we are

TaskFlex is a set of free browser tools run by Sachin, Founder of TaskFlex. Contact: josachinkumar@gmail.com.

2. Short version

TaskFlex has no accounts, no logins and no uploads to our servers. Your notes, photos, videos, passwords and PDFs are processed inside your own browser and are never sent to us.

3. What stays on your device

Settings (such as your theme) and anything you save in a tool (notes, tasks, leads, saved video links) are kept in your browser's local storage. We cannot see or recover it. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so keep your own backups.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. We do not use your camera or microphone. The clipboard is used only when you tap a Copy or Paste button.

5. Third-party services you may trigger

Video Player: if you paste a YouTube or Vimeo link, that video loads inside their player and they may set cookies and collect data under their own policies (YouTube uses the privacy-enhanced domain youtube-nocookie.com). Video to Audio: the MP3 option loads an open-source encoder (lamejs) from the jsDelivr CDN, which sees your IP address. Leads Book: Call, WhatsApp and Email buttons open your own apps; WhatsApp is run by Meta.

6. Hosting and logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive that log data.

7. Analytics, advertising and cookies

TaskFlex has no analytics of its own. To keep the tools free, TaskFlex shows ads from Adsterra on most pages. These ads load automatically. Adsterra and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure ads, and they receive technical data such as your IP address and device details. We do not pass your notes, files or tool inputs to them, and we show no ads on the Smart Notepad, Leads Book, Password Generator, or on the policy, About and Contact pages. Adsterra's own privacy policy explains how it handles data. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings or use an ad blocker if you do not want ads.

8. Legal basis (UK and EU)

Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is almost nothing to process. Where processing happens (for example the technical delivery of pages), it is based on our legitimate interest in running a working website, and in keeping the site free through advertising. If you do not want ads or advertising cookies, you can block them in your browser or with an ad blocker.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, EU GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and India's DPDP Act you may ask to access, correct or delete personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. TaskFlex holds none on its servers, but write to us and we will help.

10. Other people's details

If you store details of other people (for example in Leads Book), they stay on your device only. You are responsible for having the right to store and use them.

11. Children

TaskFlex is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the UK and EU) and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Ads, if any, will not be personalised for children.

12. Security

All processing happens in your browser over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so avoid storing highly sensitive information in browser storage.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: josachinkumar@gmail.com

15. Opting out of personalised ads

You can opt out of personalised advertising at https://www.aboutads.info and https://optout.networkadvertising.org, and you can block cookies in your browser settings.
