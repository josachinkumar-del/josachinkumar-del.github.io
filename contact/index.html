Contact

1. Email us

For questions, bug reports, tool ideas or privacy requests, write to josachinkumar@gmail.com.

2. What to include

Tell us the tool name, your device and browser, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

3. Reply time

We aim to reply within a few working days.

What to include in your message

  • The name of the tool and the page you were on.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • Your phone or computer and the browser you use.
  • A screenshot if you can add one. Please do not send private files or passwords.

When we reply

We read every message and aim to reply within 3 to 5 working days.

Bug report checklist

  1. Reload the page and try again.
  2. Try a different browser or turn off private mode.
  3. Check that you are online if the tool is 3D Studio or the MP3 maker.
  4. Note the steps that cause the problem, then email josachinkumar@gmail.com.
