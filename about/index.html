About TaskFlex

1. What TaskFlex is

TaskFlex is a growing collection of free everyday tools: notes, tasks, calculators, QR codes, image and PDF helpers and more. Everything runs in your browser.

2. Why it exists

Many tool sites ask you to upload files, sign up or accept heavy tracking. TaskFlex is built the other way round: private by design, fast on a phone, and simple to use.

3. Who builds it

TaskFlex is built and maintained by Sachin, Founder of TaskFlex.

4. Our promise

No sign-up, no watermarks, no uploads. If a tool needs the internet for something, it tells you.

5. How the tools work in your browser

Each TaskFlex tool is a small program written in JavaScript that your browser downloads once and runs locally. When you compress an image, merge photos into a PDF or count the words in a text, the work happens on your device. Your files are not uploaded, so there is nothing for us to store, sell or lose. After the first visit the site can also be saved for offline use, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

6. What we are working on

We are adding more tools that people use every day, improving the ones that already exist, and keeping the site free to use. If you have an idea, we would like to hear it.
