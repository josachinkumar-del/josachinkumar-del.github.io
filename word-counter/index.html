Questions

How is reading time worked out?

About 200 words per minute.

Is my text saved?

No.

What does it count?

Words, characters, sentences and reading time.

Does it work for Hindi text?

It counts text separated by spaces, which works for most languages including Hindi.

Can I paste a long document?

Yes. Very long text may be slow on an older phone.

Who it helps

Students with word limits, writers, social media users and translators who need counts quickly.

Examples

Check that an essay is within 500 words. Count the characters in a caption. Estimate the reading time of a speech.

Tips

Paste only the text you want counted. Other websites may count hyphenated words differently, so leave a small margin.

How it works

The tool counts words by splitting your text at spaces and line breaks, counts every character, and uses full stops, question marks and exclamation marks to count sentences. Reading time is an estimate based on an average reading speed.

Mistakes to avoid

Counting with spaces and without spaces gives different character totals, so check which one your limit means. Copy only the text you need, without extra headings or hidden notes.
