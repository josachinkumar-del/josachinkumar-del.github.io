EMI Calculator

Free EMI calculator. Enter amount, rate and years to see monthly payment, total interest and total cost.

Why TaskFlex built this

Shows your monthly loan payment as you type.

How to use it

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the yearly rate and years.
  3. Read the EMI, interest and total.

Questions

What is the formula?

EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where r is the monthly rate and n the months.

Is it exact?

It is an estimate. Banks may add fees.

What do I enter as the rate?

The yearly interest rate in percent, for example 8.

Does it work for any currency?

Yes. The maths is the same, so use any currency.

Will my bank's figure match exactly?

It can differ slightly because of rounding, fees and how the bank counts days.

Does a longer loan cost more?

A longer term lowers the monthly EMI but usually raises the total interest.

Can I use it for a car loan?

Yes. It works for any loan with equal monthly payments.

Who it helps

Anyone planning a home, car, education or personal loan, and anyone comparing offers from different banks.

Examples

A loan of 1,000,000 at 8% for 10 years gives an EMI of about 12,132.76 a month. Try the same loan for 5 years to see the monthly payment rise while the total interest falls.

Tips

Compare total interest, not only the monthly figure. Ask the lender about processing fees and insurance, because they are not part of this estimate.

How it works

The calculator turns the yearly rate into a monthly rate and applies the standard EMI formula for the number of months. It also shows the total interest, which is everything you pay minus the loan amount, and the total cost.

Mistakes to avoid

People often type the rate as a decimal such as 0.08 instead of 8, or put months in the years box. Check both before you trust the result. A floating rate can also change your EMI later.
