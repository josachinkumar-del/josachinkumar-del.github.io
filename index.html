Welcome to TaskFlex

TaskFlex is a free collection of everyday tools that run inside your web browser. You can write notes, keep a to-do list, work out a loan payment, make a QR code, shrink a photo, turn pictures into a PDF or build a strong password, all without creating an account.

Most tool websites ask you to upload your files to their servers. TaskFlex works the other way round. Your notes, photos, videos and passwords are processed on your own phone or computer and are never sent to us. That is what we mean by private by design, and it also makes the tools fast, because nothing has to travel over the internet.

Every tool is built to be simple on a small screen, with big buttons and plain words. There are no watermarks and no sign-up. Some tools, such as the 3D Studio and the MP3 maker, need internet the first time they load, and each page tells you when that applies.

TaskFlex is built by Sachin, Founder of TaskFlex, and new tools are added regularly. If a tool is missing something you need, please write to us from the Contact page. Your feedback decides what we build next.
