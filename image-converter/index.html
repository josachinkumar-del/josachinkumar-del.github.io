Questions

Which format is best?

JPEG for photos, PNG for sharp graphics, WebP for small files on the web.

Is anything uploaded?

No.

Which formats are supported?

PNG, JPEG and WebP.

Is quality lost?

JPEG and WebP can lose some detail. PNG keeps all of it.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Conversion happens on your device.

Which format should I choose?

JPEG for photos, PNG for sharp graphics and WebP when you want a smaller file.

Can I resize at the same time?

Yes. Set a maximum size before you convert.

Who it helps

People who need a photo in another format for a website or form, and designers who want a smaller WebP file.

Examples

Convert a PNG screenshot to JPEG for email. Turn a JPEG into WebP for a faster website.

Tips

JPEG suits photos, PNG suits sharp graphics and WebP is usually smaller. Set a maximum size if you also want to resize.

How it works

Your browser decodes each photo and saves it again in the format you choose, resizing it if you set a maximum size. No photo leaves your device.

Mistakes to avoid

Converting a JPEG to PNG does not bring back detail that is already lost, and it can make the file larger. Choose the format that suits where the photo will be used.
