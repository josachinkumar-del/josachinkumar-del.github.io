Cookie Policy

1. What are cookies

Cookies and similar technologies are small items stored on your device by a website.

2. What TaskFlex uses today

TaskFlex itself sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember your theme and the things you choose to save in tools. This is strictly necessary for the site to work.

3. Third parties

YouTube or Vimeo may set cookies if you play their videos in the Video Player. GitHub Pages may process technical request data.

4. Third-party ads

TaskFlex shows ads from Adsterra. These ads load automatically, and Adsterra and its partners may set cookies or use similar technologies to show and measure ads. TaskFlex itself sets no cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can use an ad blocker or the opt-out links in the Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking some may stop saved notes or settings from working.
