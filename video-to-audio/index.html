Video to Audio

Extract audio from a video file in your browser. Private: your file is never uploaded.

Why TaskFlex built this

Take the sound out of your own video or audio file, right in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Choose a video or audio file from your phone or computer.
  2. Pick MP3, WAV or compressed audio.
  3. Download the result.

Questions

Is it MP3?

Yes. Tap Make MP3. It needs internet once to load the MP3 maker. WAV and compressed audio work offline.

Is my file uploaded?

No. It never leaves your device.

Which format should I pick?

MP3 is smaller and works almost everywhere. WAV is larger and uncompressed.

Why does MP3 need internet?

The MP3 encoder is downloaded the first time. WAV works without internet.

Can I use an audio file?

Yes. Choose a video or audio file.

Will the audio quality match the original?

WAV keeps the original quality. MP3 trades some quality for a smaller file.

Who it helps

Students who want the sound of a lecture, musicians saving a clip, and anyone who wants the audio from their own video.

Examples

Turn a recorded class into an MP3 to listen to on the way home. Save a WAV copy when you want the best quality.

Tips

Use only files you own or have permission to use. MP3 needs internet the first time because its encoder is downloaded, while WAV works without internet. Long videos take longer on slow phones.

How it works

Your browser decodes the sound from the file and writes it as WAV, or as MP3 using a small encoder loaded from a fixed version. Everything happens on your device, so large files use your phone's memory and time.

Mistakes to avoid

Very long videos can run out of memory on older phones. Try a shorter clip first, close other apps and keep the screen on while it works.
