Images to PDF

Combine photos into one PDF for free. Works in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Why TaskFlex built this

Put several photos into one PDF for forms, email or printing.

How to use it

  1. Choose your images in the order you want.
  2. Tap Make PDF.
  3. Download the PDF.

Questions

What page size is used?

A4, with each image fitted inside and centred.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The PDF is built on your device.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The PDF is made on your device.

Can I change the page order?

Yes. Choose the images in the order you want before tapping Make PDF.

How do I make the PDF smaller?

Use the Image Compressor first, then make the PDF.

Can I add more than one image?

Yes. Choose several images and they become pages in order.

Is the text in the PDF searchable?

No. Each page is a picture, so the text cannot be searched.

Who it helps

Students and job seekers who must send documents as one PDF, and anyone who photographs pages with a phone.

Examples

Photograph the pages of a form, add them in order and make one PDF for email. Combine photos of your documents into a single file for an application.

Tips

Choose the images in the order you want the pages. Compress large photos first if the PDF is too big to upload.

How it works

Each image becomes one page. The PDF is built in your browser from the images you chose, in the order you chose, and is then offered as a download.

Mistakes to avoid

Very large photos make a heavy PDF that some portals reject. Check that every photo is upright and readable before you make the file, because the text on a document must be clear.
