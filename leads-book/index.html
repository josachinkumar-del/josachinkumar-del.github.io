Leads Book

Free simple lead tracker for freelancers and small shops. Call, WhatsApp or email in one tap. Data stays on your device.

Why TaskFlex built this

A simple book for customers and leads, with call, WhatsApp and email buttons.

How to use it

  1. Add a name, phone and a note.
  2. Set the status.
  3. Tap Call, WhatsApp or Email, or export a CSV.

Questions

Where are leads stored?

Only on your device. Sachin cannot recover them if you clear your browser data, so export a CSV now and then.

Can I use other people's details?

Only where the law allows. You are responsible for that.

Are my customers' details uploaded?

No. They stay in your browser on this device.

Can I move leads to a spreadsheet?

Yes. Export a CSV and open it in any spreadsheet app.

What if I clear my browser data?

The leads are deleted, so keep an exported copy.

Can I share the book with my team?

Not directly, because the data stays on one device. Export a CSV and share that file.

Does it suit any business?

Yes. It is a simple list for any business that follows up with people.

Who it helps

Small shop owners, freelancers, agents and sellers who talk to many customers and need to remember who asked for what.

Examples

Save a customer's name, number and what they wanted, set the status, then tap WhatsApp to send a follow-up. Export a CSV once a week for your own records.

Tips

Write the next step in the note so you know what to do when you open the book again. Export regularly, because the data lives only on this device.

How it works

Each lead holds a name, phone number, note and status. The Call, WhatsApp and Email buttons open the apps already on your phone, and TaskFlex never sees your messages. Export saves your leads as a CSV file.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not store sensitive details such as bank passwords or card numbers in the notes. Ask for permission before you message people, and follow the rules that apply to your business.
