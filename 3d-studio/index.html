Drive and explore a Bentley Continental GT in 3D.

Why TaskFlex built this

This is an interactive 3D model of a Bentley Continental GT grand tourer that runs entirely in your browser. We built the model ourselves, and we are giving it away free so you can use it in your own game or project. You can drive the car around, switch on the lights, open the doors, bonnet and boot, and look inside the cabin. Nothing is uploaded and nothing needs to be installed.

How to use it

  1. Open the page and wait a few seconds while the model loads. The first visit needs internet.
  2. Press Start to switch the engine on, then use the arrow buttons to steer, accelerate and reverse. Use Handbrake to stop.
  3. Tap Doors, Windows, Bonnet or Boot to open parts of the car, Inside to look around the cabin, and Chase to follow the car from behind.
  4. Try Lights, Ind, Hazard, Horn and Wipers, pick a body colour, press Photos to see the reference photos, and press Model to download the car.

Questions

Is anything uploaded?

No. The model runs on your device.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in a modern phone browser. Use the on-screen buttons to drive.

Why does it need internet?

The 3D engine (three.js) is downloaded the first time you open the page. If you are offline you will see a message asking you to connect and try again.

Can I download this 3D car?

Yes. Press the Model button. It saves the car as a ready-to-use three.js file called bentley-gt-model.js in your Downloads folder, free of charge.

Can I use it in my own game?

Yes. We made the model ourselves and offer it free, so you do not need to ask us or pay us. Credit to TaskFlex is welcome but not required.

Why do I see a short message when I download?

It is a short sponsored message that helps keep TaskFlex and this free model online. Your file starts downloading as soon as you press the button.

Use it in your game

Press Model to download the car, then add the file to your own three.js project. Build the car with the function it contains, change its colour, and drive it, open its doors or switch its lights in your own way. The car faces forward along the Z axis and one unit is one metre, so it fits into most games without resizing.

Tips

If the car runs slowly, close other browser tabs. Landscape mode gives you more room for the buttons on a phone.
