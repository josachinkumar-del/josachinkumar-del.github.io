TaskFlex

Smart Notepad

Free online notepad that saves automatically on your device. No sign-up, nothing uploaded. Download your note as a text file.

Why TaskFlex built this

Keep many notes in one place with search, pinning and checklists. Nothing needs a sign-in, and notes save as you type.

How to use it

  1. Tap New note and start typing.
  2. Use Pin, Checklist or Date to organise.
  3. Use Backup all notes to keep a copy.

Questions

Is my note uploaded?

No. It stays on your device.

Will it be there tomorrow?

Yes, unless you clear your browser data. Use Backup all notes or the Backup button on the home page.

Where are my notes stored?

In your browser's local storage on this device. They are never sent to a server.

Will I lose notes if I clear my browser?

Yes, clearing site data deletes them, so make a backup first.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded it can be saved for offline use.

Can I write in Hindi?

Yes. You can type in any language your keyboard supports.

Is there a limit on notes?

The limit depends on your browser's storage space, which is usually enough for a very large number of short notes.

Who it helps

Students, shopkeepers, writers and anyone who needs a quick place for lists, ideas and reminders without creating an account.

Examples

Keep a grocery checklist and tick items off in the shop. Pin your weekly plan so it stays on top. Write a draft message, copy it and send it later.

Tips

Use Backup all notes before you clear your browser data or change phone, because notes live only on this device. Short, clear titles make search faster.

How it works

Notes are saved in your browser's local storage as you type, so there is no Save button to forget. Search looks through your notes on your device. Pinned notes stay at the top and checklists let you tick items off.

Mistakes to avoid

Do not treat the notepad as the only copy of something important. Browser data can be cleared by cleaning apps or lost when you change phone, so back up notes you cannot afford to lose.
